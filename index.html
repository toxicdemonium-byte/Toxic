05 — Our Story

Built on a kitchen table, scaled by hand

Verde & Oak started in 2015 in a one-room studio with a single kick-wheel and a stack of reclaimed oak. The goal was simple: make the everyday objects — bowls, lamps, throws, side tables — that people actually reach for, and make them well enough that no one ever needs to replace them.

Today we're a team of fourteen makers working across ceramics, textiles, and woodwork. We still test every glaze recipe by eating off it for a month, and every textile gets washed forty times before it ships, so the "broken in" feeling starts on day one.

Honest materials

Stoneware, linen, and FSC-certified oak — nothing else.

Made slowly, on purpose

Most pieces take 5–14 days from studio to packing.

Designed to be repaired

Free glaze touch-ups and re-oiling, for life.
